Our Story

Over 35 years in the Petaluma community

It all started in 1986. The Tortuga Ceramic Studio has been part of the Petaluma community for decades, offering collaborative studio space in a rustic barn on the outskirts of town.

Tortuga was originally started by Dorothy Winer, a ceramic artist specializing in pit firing, who set up the studio and began teaching there. When Dorothy moved out of state, the studio was taken over by Diana Crain from 1997 until 2019. Diana remains available to give classes and workshops.

In 2019, three Tortuga members — Kathleen Brown, Paul DeMarco, and James Okumura — formed an LLC to purchase the studio. Paul is now carrying on the Tortuga tradition as the sole owner.

Watch this site for occasional special workshops given at Tortuga by Diana and others such as Gerald Arrington and Bill Geisinger.

Join Us

For the amateur potter to the advanced ceramic artist

Tortuga provides a well-equipped collaborative workspace with a wide selection of glazes and clays for high-fire ceramics — three group studio sessions per week, independent study, and occasional workshops.

🏺

Studio Access

Three regular group studio times each week, plus open studio access anytime after your first group session. Technical assistance is available during group times.

🔥

Clay & Firings

Custom cone 10 clay bodies available for purchase. Glaze and firing costs are included in the clay purchase price. We primarily fire using a downdraft gas kiln, with occasional pit firings.

📅

Workshops

Special workshops open to both members and non-members, charged individually. Taught by local artists including Diana Crain, Gerald Arrington, and Bill Geisinger.

Flexible Membership Fees

Fees are charged monthly, with options ranging from a single month to a discounted six-month package. Non-members are welcome for special workshops on a per-event basis — some workshops may include additional materials fees.
